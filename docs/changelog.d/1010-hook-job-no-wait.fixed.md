**The postgres add-on no longer waits minutes for the deletion of its finished bucket-wait Job (#1010).** The wait Jobs (bucket and backup plugin) carried `HookSucceeded`, so Argo CD waited for the foreground deletion of the finished Job and its pod before it went on; on a first prod sync the add-on stayed OutOfSync for about 8 minutes. The Jobs now carry only `BeforeHookCreation` and `ttlSecondsAfterFinished: 300`, so Kubernetes removes them in the background. **Action:** re-render with `/platform:update`.
