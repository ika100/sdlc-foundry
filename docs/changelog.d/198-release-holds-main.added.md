**`cplat platform release` now holds `main` against every cplat merge on the machine until the tag is pushed (#198).** The releases 5.21.0 and 5.22.0 had to start again because other merges moved `main` while the release PR waited for its CI or for your confirmation. Now the release sets a hold before its first check, waits for a merge already running to end, and merges through its own hold; merges started meanwhile (`ship land`, `pr wait --merge`, the dispatcher) wait and go on when the hold ends. The hold ends after the tag push, on an error and on Ctrl-C; at a `CONFIRM:` stop it stays for 2 hours, and the stop names the time. A second release in the same repo is refused while one runs. `cplat platform hold [--clear]` shows or lifts the hold ([ADR-089](../adr/089-every-merge-into-main-holds-the-repo-merge-lock.md), [spec 198](../specs/198-a-platform-release-holds-main-through/spec.md)). Merges by hand or from another machine are not held.
