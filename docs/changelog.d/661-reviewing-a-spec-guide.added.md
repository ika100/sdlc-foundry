**A guide to reviewing a spec before you approve it ([#661](https://github.com/ika100/sdlc-foundry/pull/661)).** Approval is the one decision the platform cannot take for you, but nothing said what to check in it. The new guide [Reviewing a spec](../REVIEWING-A-SPEC.md) gives a reading order that lets you stop at the first wrong part, one question per part, and red flags for acceptance criteria, including a criterion that changes what an earlier spec promised (#477). The quickstart links to it at the approval step.
