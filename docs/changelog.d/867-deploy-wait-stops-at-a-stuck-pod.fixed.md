**The deploy wait stops at a stuck pod and asks Argo CD for a refresh when it starts (#867).** `cplat system wait`, the deploy wait of `cplat component ship` and the staging wait of `cplat instance promote` polled until their timeout while a pod of the new version sat in `ImagePullBackOff`, and asked Argo CD for a refresh only after 45 s. Now a container of the version waited for that stays in `ImagePullBackOff`, `ErrImagePull`, `InvalidImageName` or `CrashLoopBackOff` for more than 60 s ends the wait at once as RED (exit 1, escalated like a failed sync), naming the pod, the reason, the image and the last event, and saying when the image tag does not exist in the registry. Every wait asks Argo CD for a hard refresh of the apps behind the revision right away, and once more after 45 s.
