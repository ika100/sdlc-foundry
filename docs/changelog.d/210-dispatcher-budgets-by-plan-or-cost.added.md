**The dispatcher budgets by plan limits or by cost, no longer in raw tokens (spec 210).** `budget.mode` is `cost` (the default) or `plan`. Mode `cost` caps the US dollars of a day (`budget.usd_per_day`) and of a month (`budget.usd_per_month`), priced from the price table. Mode `plan` paces by the limits of your subscription: it aims at 92 % of the weekly limit by its reset, stops starting units at 80 % of the 5-hour window, holds until the reset after a usage-limit message, and spends a spare last day on filler work (held P2 specs, `budget.plan.filler_tasks`, up to 3 retro drafts that stay open for you). `cplat dispatch sensor install` reads the limits from the status line of your interactive sessions and keeps the status line you had.

A token cap tracks neither money nor a subscription, so a count of tokens said little about what a day cost or how much of the week was left. For an existing policy, `budget.tokens_per_day` still works in mode `cost`, with a deprecation warning in `plan` and `status`. `start` and `resume` now ask for `budget.usd_per_day` (or the deprecated key); set it, or switch to `budget.mode: plan` on a subscription.
