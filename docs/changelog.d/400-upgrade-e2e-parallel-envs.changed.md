**The upgrade e2e applies dev, staging and prod in parallel in its setup and backup passes, about 13 minutes faster (#400).** Two of its passes applied the three environments one after another and waited until each was healthy (setup 624 s, backups 579 s of a 35-minute run). `tests/e2e/upgrade.sh` now starts all three at once and waits for all of them; each line carries `[dev]`, `[staging]` or `[prod]`, each environment keeps its log in the work directory, and a failure names every environment that failed with its failure lines, the end of its log and the cluster diagnostics once. The major-upgrade steps stay in order (dev, then staging, then prod). `E2E_UPGRADE_SEQUENTIAL=1` applies the environments one after another as before, for a runner short of CPU or memory.
